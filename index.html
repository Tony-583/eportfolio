Projects

Sensor Development and Implementation Pipeline - Clarkson Unversity REU Program

An Undergrad research ongoing experience to remove phosphate from water solutions that harms the ecosystem and reusing it as a fertilizer that helps for agriculture use. Mentored by Dr. Silvana Andreescu, I worked with her and her grad students to develop a material that can absorb phosphate, a chemical compound that are harmful in the water systems. At the same time, billions and billions of waste printed papers are everywhere throughout the world. In those papers, there is a special compound known as cellulose that can detect phosphate and remove them from the sample. So, cellulose is extracted from waste printed papers and then functionalized to increase the amount of absorption of phosphate. Analyzed with various instrument to test the effect, this cost-effective method can be used as a fertilizer that benefits both the ecosystem and the economy.
